Process Explanation

1. Voting: Ballots (chips) drop through the pegboard to their 1st choice candidate.

2. Tally: If no candidate has >50%, the candidate with the fewest votes is eliminated.

3. Transfer: The eliminated candidate's trapdoor opens. Their ballots cascade down to their next valid preference.
