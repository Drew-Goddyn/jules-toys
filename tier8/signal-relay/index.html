How to Play

Click or tap grid cells to place optical elements, then route colored beams from each source to its matching target.

  • Click empty cell: cycles through / → \ → ◆ → ■ → empty
  • / Mirror: reflects beam right-angle (↗ incoming → ← outgoing)
  • \ Mirror: reflects beam right-angle opposite (↘ incoming → ← outgoing)
  • ◆ Prism: splits beam into two perpendicular beams
  • ■ Block: absorbs the beam, stops propagation
